## Public eligibility facts
Transifex publicly states that it provides its platform free for eligible Open Source projects. Its public Open Source page describes eligible projects as having publicly available source code under an OSI-approved license and no funding, revenue, or commercialization model. The pricing FAQ repeats the no-funding/revenue/commercialization requirement and directs uncertain organizations to contact Transifex.

The public help center states that after account signup, the user creates an organization that contains the localization projects. Transifex also documents file-based projects for standard localization files that are uploaded, translated, and pulled back into the development workflow.
