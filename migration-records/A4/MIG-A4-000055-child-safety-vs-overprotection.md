## Discovery and boundary decision

Before Claim #167, the canonical key, proposed slug, Arabic/English synonyms and the concepts of overprotection/parental overprotection were checked in GitHub Issues, the central migration progress document, and Supabase. No competing canonical was found.

A4 owns the page because the canonical is about a parenting practice: balancing child safety, supervision and age/ability-appropriate independence. It deliberately does not diagnose parental anxiety. If a parent's fear is severe, persistent and functionally impairing, that diagnostic subject belongs to A1.

## Legacy/history audit

The verified legacy page is `quick-info/child-protection-vs-parental-anxiety/index.html`. Its concise comparison intent was retained, but the page was not copied. Generic emergency boilerplate, repeated diagnostic disclaimers, platform/GTM shell content and irrelevant structured-data citations to ADHD and autism were removed. Git history showed the same broad Quick Information long-form, shell, metadata, Discover/sitemap and GTM batch updates rather than distinct historical canonicals.

## Rebuild and evidence

The article was rebuilt around proportional risk, developmental independence, supervision, low-risk mistakes, graded support, school responsibility, digital monitoring, parent agreement, adolescence, play, and explicit red flags requiring more—not less—adult intervention.

Evidence was drawn from CDC positive-parenting resources across developmental stages, CDC guidance on adolescent independence and parental monitoring, UNICEF guidance on independent/free play and adolescent risk-taking, and AAP/HealthyChildren guidance on growing independence. The article distinguishes discomfort from danger and emphasizes that independence is not neglect.
