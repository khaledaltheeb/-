4. **WHO Handbook for Guideline Contextualization** — 2023 official methods/implementation handbook; no clinical sample.
5. **GRADE Handbook / GRADEpro** — foundational methodological handbook, available edition 2013; its age is explicitly bounded and it is supplemented by newer Cochrane/WHO methods.

The rebuilt page distinguishes risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication bias; explains that certainty is outcome-specific; separates absolute/relative effect from certainty; and explains why recommendations also consider benefits/harms, values/preferences, resources, equity, acceptability and feasibility.
