# SEO preservation invariants

This validation round treats the existing public inventory as monotonic and non-destructive.

- Published pages must not be deleted, unpublished, moved out of their publication window, or changed from `robots_index=true` to `false` by SEO work.
- Every semantic SEO profile must produce exactly 50 topical signals and 50 search-intent signals internally without synthetic numbered filler or hidden page copy.
- Public `meta keywords` remain deliberately concise because Google does not use the tag as a ranking signal; the full 100-signal inventory is an editorial/query-coverage instrument.
- Generic recovery language must not by itself classify a mental-health page as addiction; route-family and explicit domain signals take precedence.
- Every live indexable non-condition database page belongs to the general content sitemap safety net; `legacy_migration` metadata must never exclude it.
- Indexable condition records belong to the encyclopedia sitemap partition.
- Dedicated sitemaps may overlap the general safety net when useful; overlapping discovery is preferable to silently dropping an indexable published page.
- Static and generated indexable routes must have literal, generated-family, or database-backed sitemap coverage.
- Network instability in a live preservation check may trigger retries or an independent paged-row count, but it must never be converted into a successful zero/unknown count.
- Deterministic architecture contracts are separated from live inventory gates; Cloudflare validation, the full quality workflow, and production cutover all require the live preservation gate before build/deploy.
- Review attribution requires a recorded review timestamp; a named reviewer is preserved when recorded, otherwise the Rawafid review team may be used only as the institutional fallback for that recorded review.
- Passing validation never authorizes hiding, deleting, unpublishing, or changing the canonical identity of existing published content.
