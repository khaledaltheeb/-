# Medical Decision Science release — 2026-08-29

## Trigger

The release was initiated after inbound correspondence from the Society for Asia Medical Decision Science (SAMDS), Hostinger thread `INBOX:337`, highlighted a genuine Arabic/MENA content gap around medical decision science, shared decision making, risk communication, uncertainty, preference-sensitive care, and Asian-context decision making.

SAMDS/ISMDS links were used as discovery and collaboration sources only. Scientific claims were independently verified against authoritative guidance, systematic reviews, research frameworks, and peer-reviewed literature. No blanket translation, adaptation, or republication rights were assumed.

## Rights and reuse controls

- Do not treat a public download as permission to translate or adapt.
- Ottawa Personal Decision Guides have explicit use conditions; their availability does not automatically grant Arabic translation rights.
- Decisional Conflict Scale and Decision Regret Scale are not reproduced or translated in this release.
- IPDAS 5.0 is used as a standards reference; protected wording/tools are not copied into Rawafid.
- Cochrane and other scholarly sources are cited and independently synthesized; review text, figures, and tables are not republished.
- Cross-cultural evidence from Asian-American populations is used to identify mechanisms/questions, not to infer Arab/MENA prevalence or preference rates.
