## Scientific/safety corrections

### Catatonia

The page now treats catatonia as a psychomotor syndrome across psychiatric, neurological and medical conditions rather than a schizophrenia-only phenomenon. It covers malignant catatonia, complications of immobility, delirium/non-convulsive status/medication syndromes/encephalitis differential diagnosis, structured observation, benzodiazepine and ECT treatment principles without dose instructions, supportive care, neurodevelopmental change-from-baseline, reassessment and discharge/relapse planning.

### Functional Neurological Disorder (FND)

The page now uses positive neurological signs rather than a “normal tests” exclusion model. It explains that symptoms are genuine and not intentionally produced, trauma is not required, structural neurological disease can coexist, functional seizures may coexist with epilepsy, and FND-specific rehabilitation focuses on automatic movement, attention and function. Reassessment explicitly reopens differential diagnosis when the symptom pattern changes.

### ARFID

The page now separates ARFID from ordinary selective eating, Pediatric Feeding Disorder, dysphagia, gastrointestinal disease and medically necessary dietary restriction. Low BMI is explicitly not required for important nutritional risk. It integrates medical stability, growth trajectory, refeeding risk, swallowing safety, GI/autism overlap, multidisciplinary non-coercive care, school/family planning, adult independence, reassessment and explicit evidence limits.

### Prolonged Grief Disorder (PGD)

The page prevents pathologizing grief by time alone. It distinguishes ICD-11 and DSM-5-TR timing conventions, cultural/religious context, depression/PTSD/adjustment disorder, grief-focused psychotherapy, stepped bereavement support, suicide safety, children/adolescents, traumatic loss, anniversaries/digital memories and return to roles. Reassessment measures function, safety and comorbidity rather than asking only whether grief persists.

### Hoarding Disorder

The page is now the diagnostic/treatment/safety owner, while `/care-guides/hoarding-family-communication-safety-plan/` remains a differentiated family implementation route. The encyclopedia reference covers diagnosis vs collecting/other causes, fire/falls/exits/sanitation, harm reduction, specialized CBT, acquisition, family accommodation, rights/capacity/safeguarding, older adults, housing, animal hoarding, reassessment and why surprise clean-outs are not treatment in the absence of an immediate safety requirement.
