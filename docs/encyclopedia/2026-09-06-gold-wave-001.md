# Encyclopedia Gold-Standard Wave 001 — 2026-09-06

## Scope

This wave upgrades five high-priority `/encyclopedia/` condition records in place. Canonicals were preserved; no duplicate routes were created.

The production database remains the content source of truth. This document is an audit/release record, not a substitute for the stored records.

## Production safeguards added

- `private.enforce_encyclopedia_gold_standard()` is live in production.
- The guard is opt-in via `schema_json.encyclopedia_gold_standard.version >= 1`.
- Existing legacy encyclopedia pages are not unpublished or blocked merely because they have not yet been upgraded.
- A published/indexable gold page cannot silently downgrade its gold marker.
- Gold condition records require structured blocks, traceable references, claim-to-source mappings, SEO metadata and a real pre-existing review timestamp.
- Evidence-limited conditions use an explicit reduced depth floor rather than artificial padding.

## Scientific positioning

### CTCF-related disorder

Primary clinical anchor: GeneReviews (2024). The page now separates molecular diagnosis from phenotype, explicitly states that a VUS does not establish the diagnosis, treats management as symptom-directed, adds surveillance/reassessment logic, functional and educational implications, genetic-counseling boundaries and evidence limits. Additional provenance includes ClinGen, Orphanet and Human Disease Genes.

### KPTN-related disorder

Primary clinical anchor: GeneReviews (2024), supported by the original 2014 gene-discovery paper and later clinical/translational literature. The page now distinguishes human clinical care from preclinical mTOR findings and explicitly states that mTOR-pathway experiments do not establish rapamycin or other mTOR inhibitors as clinical treatment for KPTN-related disorder.

### TAOK1-related neurodevelopmental disorder

Marked `evidence_limited=true`. The 2025 multicenter cohort (50 individuals) is used as the current major phenotype expansion, with the 2021 cohort/functional paper and earlier gene-discovery/inheritance reports. No disorder-specific treatment or surveillance guideline is claimed.

### GRIA4-related neurodevelopmental disorder

Marked `evidence_limited=true`. The page records ClinGen's 2024 Moderate gene-disease validity classification and keeps seizure/gait/behavioral features probabilistic rather than obligatory. It explicitly distinguishes pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants from VUS findings and does not invent a GRIA4-specific treatment protocol.

### Alstrom syndrome

The page was expanded as a multisystem reference anchored in GeneReviews and the multidisciplinary consensus guideline. It covers molecular diagnosis, progressive visual and hearing access needs, cardiometabolic, hepatic, renal and endocrine follow-up, multidisciplinary management, education/accessibility and autosomal-recessive counseling without manufacturing a reviewer identity.

## Provenance policy

No new human/external clinical reviewer was claimed. Existing `last_reviewed_at` values and existing reviewer names were preserved. Alstrom syndrome had a real review timestamp but no reviewer name; it remains null.

Gold metadata explicitly records `external_clinical_review_claimed=false`.

## Version baseline

These five legacy records had zero rows in `public.content_versions` before this wave. After the gold upgrade, version `1` baseline snapshots were created for each current gold record, including category/tag relation snapshots. This establishes a comparison/recovery baseline for future edits.

Important limitation: these baselines represent the post-upgrade gold state. They are not falsely described as snapshots of the pre-wave state, because no such `content_versions` snapshots existed.

## Cross-encyclopedia production preflight

- invalid encyclopedia canonicals: 0
