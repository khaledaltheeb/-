## 1. Auditory Processing Disorder (CAPD/APD)

The upgrade avoids presenting APD as a simple single-test diagnosis.

Scientific anchors:

- ASHA CAPD Practice Portal;
- British Society of Audiology APD guidance;
- 2024 scoping review of pediatric CAPD diagnosis/rehabilitation;
- 2025 review of the continuing diagnostic/protocol heterogeneity.

Key corrections:

- no universal single diagnostic gold standard is claimed;
- peripheral hearing assessment precedes central-auditory interpretation;
- language, attention, learning, memory and multilingual factors are treated as genuine differential/coexisting contributors;
- audiologist-selected batteries are individualized rather than fixed;
- direct auditory training is not described as a universal cure;
- environmental access, remote-microphone trials, compensatory strategies and direct skills work are tied to measurable functional goals;
- success includes real-world listening, communication and educational outcomes, not improvement on the practiced test alone.

## 2. Cerebral/Cortical Visual Impairment (CVI)

Primary clinical anchor: the 2024 American Academy of Pediatrics clinical report on diagnosis and care of children with cerebral/cortical visual impairment, supported by NIH, Perkins and CAST accessibility resources.

Key corrections:

- CVI is framed as brain-based visual impairment not fully explained by ocular findings;
- ocular disease may coexist and a comprehensive ophthalmic examination remains essential;
- routine vision screening may miss CVI;
- no single universally accepted diagnostic test is claimed;
- imaging is not treated as a stand-alone diagnostic result;
- medical diagnosis and functional-vision/education assessment are separated but linked;
- academic/cognitive ability must not be inferred from an inaccessible visual task;
- accommodations are individualized and measured rather than applied as a fixed CVI checklist;
- no commercial or disease-specific intervention is presented as a proven cure.

## 3. Weaver syndrome / EZH2-related overgrowth

Primary clinical anchor: GeneReviews `EZH2-Related Overgrowth`, updated in 2025.

Key corrections:

- classic Weaver syndrome is positioned within the broader EZH2-related overgrowth spectrum;
- a pathogenic/likely pathogenic heterozygous EZH2 variant establishes the molecular diagnosis; VUS does not;
- intellectual/developmental outcome is explicitly variable and is not inferred from overgrowth;
- management is supportive and manifestation-directed;
- neuroblastoma risk is described as increased but the absolute risk remains difficult to quantify;
- the 2024 AACR childhood cancer predisposition update supports neuroblastoma surveillance for EZH2-related Weaver syndrome, while GeneReviews notes international inconsistency in modality/interval/duration;
- families are therefore directed to a genetics/pediatric-oncology surveillance plan rather than a self-executed internet schedule.

## 4. Bardet-Biedl syndrome (BBS)

Scientific anchors:

- 2024 Inter-European Reference Networks consensus on improved BBS diagnostic criteria and management;
- GeneReviews BBS overview;
- current FDA and EMA regulatory information for setmelanotide/IMCIVREE;
- Orphanet and MedlinePlus for additional rare-disease/patient reference context.

Key corrections:

- BBS is treated as a genetically heterogeneous multisystem ciliopathy with age-dependent and variable expression;
- the 2024 ERN consensus is used to integrate clinical and molecular diagnosis instead of waiting for every classic feature;
- progressive retinal disease and anticipatory accessible-format/mobility planning are covered;
- renal disease is treated as a major longitudinal morbidity even when initially asymptomatic;
- metabolic, hepatic, thyroid, blood-pressure, sleep, developmental and other systems are integrated into coordinated follow-up;
- setmelanotide is accurately described as a current regulator-approved treatment for BBS-associated obesity in eligible patients in relevant jurisdictions, **not** as a cure for BBS or a treatment for retinal/renal/genetic manifestations;
- no dose or self-treatment instruction is provided; eligibility and monitoring remain specialist-led.

## 5. Cluttering canonical consolidation

### Problem found

Three published pages addressed substantially the same core Cluttering search intent:

1. `/encyclopedia/cluttering/`
2. `/encyclopedia/cluttering-communication-disorder/`
3. `/content/cluttering-fluency-disorder`

The third route already contained a strong long-form evidence resource. Expanding all three independently would have amplified internal SEO/content cannibalization.

### Decision

`/encyclopedia/cluttering/` is the canonical public owner because it is the shortest stable descriptive route for the concept.

The owner was upgraded using the strong prior Rawafid long-form material plus refreshed 2024–2026 evidence. The Arabic framing was improved to lead with `الكلام المتسارع غير المنظم (Cluttering)` rather than using `التلعثم` as if Cluttering and stuttering were synonymous.

Current owner metrics:

- 3,043 structured words;
- 101 structured blocks;
- 8 references;
- 8 claim-source mappings;
- 5 category links after merging useful taxonomy relationships from the long-form source;
- one indexable canonical owner.

Evidence includes ASHA fluency guidance, 2026 child/adolescent review, 2026 personal-impact scoping review, 2025 adult life-impact study and relevant comparative/context studies.

### No-loss handling of legacy routes

The two legacy source records were **not deleted and not archived**. The platform's published-content preservation guard correctly rejected a direct noindex attempt without an explicit preservation hold.

The supported safety mechanism was then used:

- `status=published` remains unchanged;
- `robots_index=false`;
- `robots_follow=true`;
- `schema_json.content_quality_hold.status=safety_hold_pending_review`;
- `content_quality_hold.content_preserved=true`;
- `schema_json.consolidation.canonical_owner=/encyclopedia/cluttering/`.

Therefore their content stays recoverable in production and in the pre-wave snapshots, while they no longer compete in the index.

Pre-redirect verification:

- indexable old Cluttering routes: **0**;
- indexable canonical Cluttering owners: **1**;
- indexable internal content links to either old route outside the owner metadata: **0**.

The one previously discovered internal reference to `/content/cluttering-fluency-disorder` was updated to `/encyclopedia/cluttering/`.

### Permanent redirects

`next.config.ts` adds permanent redirects for both slash and no-slash variants:

- `/encyclopedia/cluttering-communication-disorder` -> `/encyclopedia/cluttering/`
- `/encyclopedia/cluttering-communication-disorder/` -> `/encyclopedia/cluttering/`
- `/content/cluttering-fluency-disorder` -> `/encyclopedia/cluttering/`
- `/content/cluttering-fluency-disorder/` -> `/encyclopedia/cluttering/`

A dedicated `scripts/encyclopedia-canonical-consolidation-contract.mjs` is wired into the encyclopedia CI workflow so accidental removal or weakening of these redirects fails the quality gate.
