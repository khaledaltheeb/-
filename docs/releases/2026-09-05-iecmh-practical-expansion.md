# IECMH practical expansion — 2026-09-05

## Scope
Six additional Arabic IECMH resources were released through the production `content_release_gate_v6` after the original six-page IECMH reference layer.

### Released pages
- `/content/iecmh-dc05-assessment-guide` — DC:0–5 assessment literacy, five-axis overview, screening/assessment/diagnosis boundaries, DSM/ICD crosswalk context, and rights boundary.
- `/content/iecmh-consultation-early-childhood-guide` — infant and early childhood mental health consultation (IECMHC), child/family-centered and programmatic consultation, privacy, data, referral, anti-exclusion, and quality measurement.
- `/content/iecmh-home-visiting-family-guide` — voluntary early-childhood home visiting, family goals, coaching in routines, referral, CQI, supervision, fidelity, hybrid delivery, and family voice.
- `/content/iecmh-father-involvement-guide` — father involvement from pregnancy through age five, direct caregiving, co-regulation, play, paternal mental health, coparenting, nonresident fathers, disability, and service-access barriers.
- `/content/iecmh-responsive-feeding-guide` — responsive feeding, hunger/satiety cues, autonomy, pressure-free encouragement, complementary feeding, PFD/ARFID/swallowing boundaries, medical history, culture, and monitoring.
- `/content/iecmh-practical-family-professional-library` — printable/non-diagnostic observation and decision worksheets for families and professionals.

## Evidence families used
Public/authoritative sources were used and linked/cited rather than reproduced:
- ZERO TO THREE — DC:0–5 Version 2.0, public five-axis explanation, official DSM/ICD crosswalk, permissions boundary, and announced revision work.
- Center of Excellence for Infant & Early Childhood Mental Health Consultation — public IECMHC definitions, types, boundaries, and consultation guidance.
- HRSA MIECHV / HomVEE context — voluntary evidence-based home visiting, performance/CQI, model diversity, and implementation structure.
- WHO / UNICEF — Nurturing Care, Care for Child Development, complementary feeding and responsive feeding, and disability-inclusive nurturing care.
- Current peer-reviewed systematic reviews/meta-analyses for father involvement, home visiting, and responsive feeding.

## Rights and attribution boundary
- No WAIMH, ZERO TO THREE, WHO, UNICEF, HRSA, or IECMHC endorsement/approval of Rawafid is claimed.
- DC:0–5 diagnostic criteria, training tables, and protected manual text were not reproduced. Public explanatory material was synthesized in original Arabic and users are directed to the official source for professional application.
- Public sources support claims and structure; partner correspondence itself is not published.

## Release-quality snapshot
At final database audit, the full IECMH layer contained 12 published V6 editorial pages (the original six plus these six), totaling 31,773 Arabic words, 83 reference entries, 76 claim-to-source mappings, 96 FAQ items, and 120 explicit search-intent questions. All 12 met the V6 structural release contract at the time of audit.
