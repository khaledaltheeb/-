### Evidence anchors

- AAPM&R / PM&R KnowledgeNow for specialty rehabilitation pathways;
- VA/DoD and WHO service/device guidance for limb-loss rehabilitation where relevant;
- NICE rehabilitation after critical illness;
- ASCO exercise guidance during active cancer treatment;
- WHO Rehabilitation and ICF for function/activity/participation outcomes.

### Important boundaries

- Cancer rehabilitation does not publish a universal exercise prescription or fixed laboratory thresholds; exercise and precautions remain individualized to oncology treatment, bone involvement, blood counts, cardiopulmonary status, neuropathy, infection risk and the treating team.
- Post-ICU rehabilitation treats recovery as multidomain: physical weakness, respiratory and swallowing issues, cognition, sleep, mental health, family burden and return to work/community.
- Parkinson rehabilitation separates gait/freezing/falls, communication/swallowing, cognition/daily function and symptom/medication timing.
- Amputation rehabilitation is a person-centred pathway across residual-limb health, pain, mobility, prosthetic use, skin, work and long-term follow-up rather than a product catalogue.

### Low back pain / sciatica

Evidence families:
- AAPM&R Lumbar Strain, Lumbar Spondylosis and Lumbar Disc Disorders;
- NICE NG59;
- WHO 2023 non-surgical management of chronic primary low back pain;
- VA/DoD low-back-pain guideline;
- WHO ICF.

The page emphasizes clinical triage, avoiding routine imaging when it will not change management, remaining active, individualized exercise, psychosocial barriers, return to work and appropriate escalation for radicular or red-flag presentations.

### Rotator cuff / shoulder

Evidence families:
- AAPM&R Rotator Cuff Shoulder Tendon and Muscle Injuries;
- AAOS 2025 Rotator Cuff Injuries Clinical Practice Guideline;
- WHO Rehabilitation / ICF.

The page separates rotator-cuff injury from frozen shoulder, osteoarthritis, cervical/neurologic causes and other shoulder pain; it covers non-operative rehabilitation, progressive loading, overhead work/sport, injection context, surgery-selection principles and individualized postoperative rehabilitation.

### Hip osteoarthritis

Evidence families:
- AAPM&R Osteoarthritis;
- AAOS Management of Osteoarthritis of the Hip CPG, published 2023 and updated communication in 2024;
- NICE NG226;
- ACR/Arthritis Foundation osteoarthritis guidance;
- WHO ICF.

The page focuses on hip-specific function: gait, hip-spine interaction, range of motion, chair rise, stairs, walking aids, work/travel, exercise and strength, flare management, referral for total hip arthroplasty and pre/post-operative rehabilitation.

### Heart transplantation

Evidence families:
- AAPM&R Cardiac Rehabilitation Before and After Cardiac Transplantation, updated 2025-12-17;
- ISHLT Guidelines for the Care of Heart Transplant Recipients (2023);
- ISHLT Guidelines for Evaluation and Care of Cardiac Transplant Candidates (2024);
- AHA/AACVPR Core Components of Cardiac Rehabilitation (2024);
- WHO Rehabilitation / ICF.

The page addresses prehabilitation, early postoperative mobility, denervated-heart physiology and exercise monitoring, aerobic and resistance training, immunosuppression/infection considerations, nutrition, bone health, psychosocial adjustment, discharge, work/community return and long-term activity maintenance.
