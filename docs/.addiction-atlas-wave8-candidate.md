## Scientific constraints
- Wave 8 must preserve the risk scores already published for every selected substance. The release adds evidence traceability; it does not manufacture or silently recalibrate scores.
- Every reviewed cell keeps the exact published score or `null`, an independent A/B/C/U evidence grade, one or more resolvable source IDs unless grade is U, context text, and a concise rationale.
- Class-level evidence must be labeled explicitly as class-level evidence. It must never be presented as a substance-specific trial or direct pair study.
- A lower ordinal score never means safe. Scores remain within one risk dimension and must not be summed or averaged into a total danger score.
- `U` remains unresolved and therefore requires `null`.
- Missing interaction pairs remain “not reviewed yet”, never “safe”. Wave 8 does not create new interaction records merely from risk-axis evidence.
- No use-dose instructions, mixing recipes, intoxication optimization, personal probabilities, potency ratios or self-managed dangerous withdrawal schedules may be introduced.

## Required substance-specific safeguards
- Methadone cardiac evidence must retain direct FDA prescribing-information support for QT prolongation; do not represent this as a generic opioid inference.
- Tramadol neurological evidence must retain the official FDA label support for seizure risk; do not derive it only from the opioid class.
- Alcohol withdrawal medical risk must retain WHO withdrawal guidance supporting severe withdrawal, seizures/delirium and medical management.
- Buprenorphine evidence must retain SAMHSA treatment context and clearly separate its role as evidence-based OUD treatment from misuse/interaction risk.
- Nitazenes withdrawal medical risk remains `U/null` until evidence supports a responsible ordinal score. Being an emerging opioid class is not enough to manufacture a withdrawal score.
- Cychlorphine remains fully protected as Wave 7 established: all eight risk scores `null` and all eight axis evidence grades `U` until stronger evidence exists.

## Source architecture
Wave 8 adds repository-owned source registries `source-registry-v4.json` and `source-registry-v5.json`, including current official NIDA, FDA, WHO, EUDA and SAMHSA sources. Runtime must resolve these registries locally and must not fetch moving external sources at request time.

Wave 8 evidence lives in `risk-evidence-v8.json`, registered through `risk-evidence-manifest.json`. The historical vendoring script must never overwrite the repository-owned evidence manifest or post-snapshot evidence waves.
