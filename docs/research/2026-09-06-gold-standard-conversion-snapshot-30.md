## Added condition-specific strategies in pages 26–30

### NF1
Academic-domain error analysis rather than generic learning disability: 2025 meta-analyses of reading/writing/mathematics and visuospatial/visuomotor function, executive support, and medical red flags for vision/tumour/pain/hypertension/seizure changes.

### 22q11.2 deletion syndrome
Multisystem medical safety plus palate/hearing-aware speech-language assessment, working-memory/executive support, longitudinal cognitive-functional baseline, and careful psychiatric escalation based on sustained change rather than fear-driven psychosis labeling.

### Phelan-McDermid syndrome
Raw-skill growth is separated from falling standardized scores; multimodal AAC, pain/sleep/GI confounders, longitudinal adaptive tracking, and operational definition of true regression/neuropsychiatric decompensation.

### Down syndrome
Hearing, sleep, thyroid and vision are treated as access gates; expressive/receptive language, verbal-memory and executive/adaptive pathways are separated; adolescence includes structured DSRD differential assessment and adulthood includes baseline-based dementia monitoring rather than one-test diagnosis.

### Kernicterus
Motor and auditory impairment are explicitly prevented from becoming proxies for cognition. The page treats auditory neuropathy, dystonia/choreoathetosis and possible oculomotor limitations as access problems; it validates motor-light communication channels rather than assuming speech, hand response, hearing-only input or eye gaze is reliable.

## Invariant

A Gold page is not a long article. It is a current, condition-specific scientific decision system: it identifies what can hide ability, what medical/safety factors can distort performance, how to test hypotheses fairly, how to discover and develop strengths without stereotyping, how to measure real-world benefit, and when to continue, modify, stop or escalate.
