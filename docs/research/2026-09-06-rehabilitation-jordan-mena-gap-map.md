# Rehabilitation in Jordan and MENA — gap map (wave 3)

Date: 2026-09-06
Sector: `rehabilitation-functioning`
Category: `rehabilitation-mena-jordan`
Production: https://healthrenewal.org

## Baseline

Before this wave, `rehabilitation-mena-jordan` had **0** published/indexable pages. The broader rehabilitation sector had extensive condition-specific and profession-specific content, but users in Jordan still lacked a local navigation layer answering practical questions about access, devices, discharge continuity and how rehabilitation systems should be organized in the region.

## Editorial decision

Do not fill the local category by translating international clinical pages or publishing a fragile list of facilities. Build durable decision pathways that separate:

1. **stable international evidence** — rehabilitation principles, ICF, workforce competencies, wheelchair/device service standards;
2. **country-specific facts that can change** — service availability, coverage, eligibility, referral processes and local rights;
3. **official verification points** — Jordan Ministry of Health, Higher Council for the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, relevant Jordanian authorities, and provider/payer confirmation at the time of use.

Local pages therefore avoid unsupported claims that a specific benefit, facility or device is currently available to every user.

## Jordan-specific verification boundary

The category intentionally distinguishes durable guidance from volatile local facts.

### Safe durable claims
- Jordan Ministry of Health and HCD are official verification points for health-system and disability-rights information within their respective mandates.
- Rehabilitation should be organized around function, participation, continuity and person-centred goals.
- Assistive technology requires service provision, fitting/training and follow-up — not product distribution alone.

### Facts requiring re-verification at the time of use
- whether a specific provider currently offers a profession/service;
- waiting times;
- payer/insurance eligibility and visit limits;
- device coverage and replacement schedules;
- prices;
- provider hours and contact details;
- local benefit or program eligibility;
- legal/administrative procedures that may change.

A future provider directory should record the source and **verification date** for every volatile field.

## Rights and attribution boundary

- WHO, Jordanian government bodies and professional organizations are evidence sources only. Citation does not imply endorsement, accreditation, certification or review of Rawafid/Health Renewal.
- Do not present generalized health information as an official Jordanian referral rule unless the responsible Jordanian authority is cited directly.
- Do not copy protected assessment instruments, scoring keys or proprietary clinical materials.
- For legal-rights pages, use exact current Jordanian legislation/official instructions before making entitlement or procedure claims.
