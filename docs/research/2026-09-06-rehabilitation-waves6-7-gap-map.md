After repeated clinical expansion, a fresh live count showed that page creation was no longer the only issue: several previously authored pages still existed in production as drafts and therefore were absent from category coverage. The correct next step was to repair those release-contract gaps before adding more content.

## New evidence-led gaps closed

### Lung transplantation
Reason for separate pathway:
- general COPD and ILD rehabilitation do not cover transplant candidacy, frailty, prehabilitation, post-transplant mobility, immunosuppression and graft-specific follow-up.

High-value current evidence:
- AAPM&R transplant rehabilitation;
- ISHLT 2021 candidate-selection consensus;
- **ISHLT 2026 lung-transplant frailty consensus**;
- ISHLT perioperative/ICU guidance;
- ATS pulmonary rehabilitation.

### Liver / kidney transplantation
Reason for dedicated multisystem pathway:
- general adult rehabilitation does not cover dialysis-related activity, hepatic sarcopenia/cognition, immunosuppression, abdominal transplant recovery and transplant-specific return to function.

High-value current evidence:
- AAPM&R liver/kidney transplantation;
- 2026 solid-organ prehab/rehab review;
- 2026 kidney-transplant exercise meta-analysis;
- liver-transplant exercise and survivorship systematic reviews.

### Interstitial lung disease / pulmonary fibrosis
Reason for separate pathway:
- COPD does not capture the characteristic exertional desaturation, restrictive physiology, autoimmune comorbidity and different progression patterns of ILD.

High-value current evidence:
- AAPM&R intrinsic restrictive lung disease, updated May 2026;
- ATS strong recommendation for pulmonary rehabilitation in ILD;
- 2024–2025 ILD pulmonary-rehabilitation systematic reviews/meta-analyses.

### Burn rehabilitation
Reason for separate pathway:
- emergency category previously contained evacuation/contact and digital-rehabilitation content, but no end-to-end burn-rehabilitation pathway.

High-value evidence:
- AAPM&R Burn Rehabilitation;
- ISBI Burn Care Guidelines;
- 2024 overview of burn-rehabilitation systematic reviews;
- 2024 functional-independence systematic review;
- 2025 conservative scar-management review;
- WHO emergency rehabilitation / EMT rehabilitation.

The page explicitly reflects evidence uncertainty where systematic-review quality is low instead of presenting every common intervention as high-certainty practice.

## Next-gap rule

Future expansion should use this order:

1. re-count live category coverage;
2. search semantically for existing reference pathways;
3. repair any draft/unindexed high-quality page before creating a replacement;
4. create a new page only for a distinct user journey;
5. use current authoritative evidence and preserve uncertainty;
6. release only through V6.

Likely next areas after another live check:
- polytrauma as a distinct rehabilitation pathway;
- psychiatric/psychosocial rehabilitation pathways beyond pain/trauma;
- structured rehabilitation-service pathways and transitions;
- emergency/disaster rehabilitation at system level;
- other AAPM&R topics only when they close a live Arabic gap rather than merely increase page count.
