## Purpose
Build a durable Arabic reference center for Fragile X syndrome (FXS) and FMR1-associated conditions. The center is not part of pediatric oncology. It is a first-class section inside the special-needs / inclusive-education sector, with cross-classification into genetic syndromes, neurodevelopment, communication, sensory regulation, education, family navigation, healthcare access and transition-to-adulthood where appropriate.

## Editorial contract
- Arabic-first, original evidence-led synthesis; no copied protected prose.
- One primary search intent per indexed page; enrich/cross-list instead of creating duplicate pages.
- Release only through Rawafid V6 content gate: >=2500 useful Arabic words for editorial pages, >=8 H2, >=4 H3, >=6 structured FAQs, >=8 explicit search-intent questions, >=5 authoritative references including >=2 primary/guideline/systematic-review sources, claim-source mapping, originality review, taxonomy confidence >=0.9, exact central disclaimer contract, complete SEO metadata.
- High-risk genetic/medical claims require source-level mapping and a 14-day review target; general section review cycle 30 days.
- Separate full mutation FXS from premutation-associated conditions. Do not describe a premutation carrier as having FXS without the appropriate molecular/clinical basis.
- Separate standard care from investigational treatments. A registered or completed clinical trial is not proof of efficacy.
- Separate human clinical evidence from animal/cellular/mechanistic work and from theses/dissertations.
- Do not infer prognosis from CGG repeat number alone. Account for sex, methylation, size/methylation mosaicism, X-inactivation and individual/environmental factors.

## Evidence hierarchy
Tier A — clinical/technical anchors:
- GeneReviews: FMR1 Disorders, last revision 2024-05-16. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK1384/
- ACMG: Laboratory testing for fragile X, 2021 revision. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41436-021-01115-y
- EMQN best practice guidelines for molecular testing/reporting of FXS and associated disorders. https://www.nature.com/articles/ejhg2014185
- CDC Fragile X Syndrome pages; current public-health material updated in 2026. https://www.cdc.gov/fragile-x-syndrome/
- AAP: Health Supervision for Children With Fragile X Syndrome; reaffirmed 2023. https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/127/5/994/64903/Health-Supervision-for-Children-With-Fragile-X
- Fragile X Clinical & Research Consortium / National Fragile X Foundation treatment recommendations. https://fragilex.org/resources/treatment/

Tier B — systematic and high-level reviews:
- Fragile X Syndrome Across the Lifespan: A Systematic Review (2025), PMID 40004478.
- Pharmacological management of fragile X syndrome: systematic review of blinded placebo-controlled trials (2024), PMID 38393835.
- Psychiatric difficulties in females with fragile X syndrome: systematic review/meta-analysis (2026), PMID 42487767.
- Autonomic function in Fragile X syndrome: systematic review (2026), PMID 42028919.
- Reviews on somatic instability/mosaicism and full-mutation versus premutation biology (2025).
- FXAND systematic review and International FMR1 premutation conference recommendations for premutation-associated conditions.

Tier C — clinical trials / current research:
- NCT05163808 — zatolmilast/BPN14770 adolescent FXS trial, completed 2025; record updated 2026.
- NCT05358886 — adult male phase 3 zatolmilast study.
- NCT05367960 — zatolmilast open-label extension; ongoing record.
- NCT04977986 — RECONNECT transdermal cannabidiol phase 3, completed 2025; registry result status must be distinguished from peer-reviewed efficacy publication.
- Recent randomized metformin trial, PMID 41291951; interpret from full published results, not title or abstract assumptions.
- Lovastatin/minocycline crossover RCT, PMID 39651602.

## Ongoing evidence maintenance
- Recheck GeneReviews/ACMG/FXCRC and major systematic reviews each review cycle.
- Track ClinicalTrials.gov records by NCT ID and distinguish recruitment/completion from posted results and peer-reviewed publication.
- Add theses only from identifiable institutional repositories and record institution, degree, year, persistent identifier, population/model, methods and limitations.
- Retraction/status checks are mandatory before using new literature in an indexed page.
- Never represent NFXF/FXCRC linking as endorsement by those organizations.
