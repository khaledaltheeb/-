# منصة روافد — Visual Principles

- Public experience is editorial and institutional, not an admin dashboard converted into a website.
- Off-white and white are the primary surfaces; Rawafid teal is the identity anchor.
- Gold, coral, lilac, green and blue are controlled accents with explicit semantic roles.
- Whitespace and typography carry hierarchy; cards are used only where grouping or action benefits from them.
- Sector identity is expressed with a limited accent, never by replacing the global brand system.
- Desktop, tablet and mobile are first-class layouts. Mobile navigation and safe areas are part of the shell contract.
- Motion is subtle and optional; reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential movement.
- User intent determines information architecture: search and guided discovery precede exhaustive navigation.
- Public pages favor readability, trust, references and progressive disclosure over dense control surfaces.
- Admin/portal surfaces may be denser, but they reuse the same tokens and accessibility foundation.
