Subject: Optional bilingual family-reading companion for AFRC

Dear Julia,

Thank you again for the guidance. As we prepare Rawafid's participation, we have also begun developing a small bilingual companion resource for caregivers that could add practical value around the reading experience without changing or reproducing BookSmart content.

The working concept is **Read Together, Grow Together — A Family Guide to Reading, Emotional Wellbeing, Safety & Belonging** (Arabic and English). It translates evidence-informed principles around responsive shared reading into short caregiver prompts on emotional wellbeing, resilience, healthy comparison, gratitude, body safety and boundaries, social confidence, family belonging, autonomy, and inclusive reading.

We are preparing it at our own editorial responsibility and cost. Our intention would be to keep BookSmart as the reading destination and use the companion only to help caregivers make the shared-reading moment more interactive and supportive.

Before taking the campaign edition any further, I wanted to ask whether this direction would be useful from Worldreader's perspective. If so, we could share a short preview and adapt the campaign-specific references according to whichever level is most appropriate for you:

- a simple Rawafid companion linked alongside the campaign;
- a version where Worldreader reviews only the BookSmart / AFRC-specific references and wording; or
- co-branding, if Worldreader considers the resource sufficiently useful and appropriate.

We would not use the Worldreader logo, imply endorsement, or describe the guide as a Worldreader resource without your written approval.

If the idea fits what you are trying to achieve with family engagement, we would be very happy to prepare a concise preview for your review.
