## Purpose

Rawafid accepted Worldreader’s invitation to participate in the **2026 All Families Reading Challenge (AFRC)** as a **Family Engagement Champion**. This package consolidates the work prepared on Rawafid’s side so Worldreader can review the proposed companion concept and confirm the remaining campaign-operational details before any public co-branded release.

## Current campaign facts used in the preparation

The current Worldreader campaign pages state that:

- AFRC runs throughout **September 2026** in connection with International Literacy Day.
- The global virtual launch is **2 September 2026**.
- BookSmart is free for participating families, with no paywalls, ads, or hidden subscriptions stated on the current English campaign page.
- The campaign is available in **Arabic, English, French, Kiswahili, and Spanish**.
- Families are encouraged to read **10 children’s books** and complete **5 Brain Building Moments through Vroom Tips** during the month.
- The current Arabic campaign page says the free BookSmart collection and activities are available for families with children **up to age 8**.
- Julia’s invitation to Rawafid also noted that the **10 selected Challenge books are particularly geared toward ages 3–5**. Rawafid therefore proposes wording that preserves both facts rather than flattening them into a single age claim.
- Worldreader’s current English page describes support for community partners including co-branded social templates, flyers, campaign graphics, recognition on the Challenge event page, and post-campaign impact use.
- The current English page asks partners, after the Challenge, to help showcase **2–3 participating families** with a photo and short experience. Rawafid will not solicit identifiable child/family stories for onward sharing until the consent/release workflow is aligned with Worldreader guidance.

Authoritative campaign pages:

- English: https://www.worldreader.org/all-families-reading-challenge-2026/
- Arabic: https://www.worldreader.org/all-families-reading-challenge-2026-arabic/

## Rawafid deliverables prepared

### 1. Arabic campaign landing page — preparatory branch

Route:

`/campaigns/all-families-reading-challenge-2026`

The preparatory page:

- attributes AFRC and BookSmart clearly to Worldreader;
- links families to the official campaign and BookSmart destination;
- adds practical shared-reading guidance without reproducing Worldreader books;
- includes inclusive reading guidance for children who communicate through speech, AAC, gesture, pointing, signing, or other modes;
- keeps digital reading framed as shared interaction rather than passive screen exposure;
- includes privacy language separating reading participation from optional family-story/photo sharing;
- uses privacy-preserving outbound analytics without child PII;
- remains intentionally `index: false` and is not merged to production pending Worldreader review and operational confirmation.

### 2. Bilingual companion manuscript

Working title:

**Read Together, Grow Together — A Family Guide to Reading, Emotional Wellbeing, Safety & Belonging**

Arabic and English are structured in parallel. The companion does **not** reproduce BookSmart content. It is designed as a Rawafid-authored caregiver layer around the shared-reading moment, covering:

- responsive shared reading;
- emotional language and repair;
- resilience without pressure or shame;
- gratitude/contentment without moralizing;
- healthy comparison and self-worth;
- body safety, boundaries, familiar-person safety, touch context, and disclosure;
- social confidence, play entry, and assertiveness;
- family belonging, respect, autonomy, and disagreement;
- inclusive reading and communication differences;
- repetition and digital shared reading;
- caregiver wellbeing.

The manuscript is intentionally conservative: it does not claim that reading treats, prevents, or diagnoses mental-health/developmental conditions, and it does not assign children responsibility for preventing abuse.

## Points requested from Worldreader before public release

Rawafid would appreciate confirmation on the following:

1. **Age wording:** may Rawafid state that the broader BookSmart AFRC offer is for families with children up to age 8, while noting that the 10 selected Challenge books are primarily geared to ages 3–5?
2. **Attribution:** is a Rawafid-specific referral link, UTM, QR code, or other attribution method available, or should Rawafid use the generic official BookSmart campaign link?
3. **Designation:** may Rawafid publicly use the English designation **Family Engagement Champion**, and does Worldreader have a preferred Arabic rendering?
4. **Logo / co-branding:** what exact logo format should Rawafid send for the Challenge page, and is there a preferred treatment for Worldreader/BookSmart identity on Rawafid’s own landing page?
5. **Companion review level:** would Worldreader prefer (a) a Rawafid-only companion with factual campaign attribution, (b) review limited to Worldreader/BookSmart/AFRC-specific wording, or (c) consideration of co-branding after review?
6. **Family stories:** does Worldreader have a standard parental consent/photo/story release or safeguarding template for the requested post-Challenge family stories?
7. **Tracking/reporting:** which regional participation/reach metrics should Rawafid expect after the Challenge, and is there a preferred reporting format for Family Engagement Champions?

## Release rule

Until those items are resolved, Rawafid will not:

- present the companion as Worldreader-authored, reviewed, sponsored, or endorsed;
- use Worldreader/BookSmart logos outside the clearly permitted supplied campaign assets;
- describe a generic campaign link as Rawafid-specific tracking;
- collect identifiable family stories for onward sharing;
- merge the campaign landing page to production or switch it to indexable status.
