# Addiction Atlas continuity contract

Target repository: `khaledaltheeb/-`.

## Baseline that must not regress
- 54 substance/family records.
- 34 editorial, indexable comparison pages.
- At least 6 explicitly reviewed interaction pairs; absence from the matrix never means a combination is safe.
- 8 independent risk dimensions: acute toxicity, overdose, dependence, medical withdrawal risk, neuro harm, cardio harm, respiratory harm, polysubstance risk.
- At least 18 substances with claim-level/axis-level evidence tracing, covering all 8 dimensions for each reviewed substance (144 evidence cells at the 2026-08-26 checkpoint).
- Every reviewed evidence cell retains the published score, an independent A/B/C/U evidence grade, exposure/context text, rationale text and one or more resolvable source IDs.
- A `U` evidence grade must keep the risk value `null`; uncertainty must never be converted into a guessed number.
- At least 7 curated epidemiology records and 6 mortality records, each retaining year, geography, metric definition and a resolvable source ID.
- Arabic + English display names on every substance page.
- Arabic transliterations, aliases and misspellings are for search resolution/metadata only; never publish them as hidden keyword stuffing.
- No use-dose instructions, mixing recipes, intoxication optimization, or self-managed dangerous withdrawal schedules.
- Every substance record keeps direct source URLs and evidence grade A/B/C/U.
- Population statistics must never be converted into personal risk probabilities, and category-level numbers must never be assigned to an individual substance without an explicit source-level mapping.
- Interaction evidence must distinguish direct-pair evidence from class-to-substance or class-to-class evidence. Never describe a class warning as if a pair-specific clinical trial established it.
- No existing published page may be deleted, hidden or converted to `noindex` as part of atlas work.

## Scientific display rules
- Substance pages with axis-level review must expose the evidence behind each reviewed risk dimension, including evidence grade, context, rationale and named source links.
- Substance pages without axis-level review must say so explicitly; they must not silently inherit a stronger confidence label.
- The overall substance evidence grade is not a substitute for axis-level evidence. Different dimensions may have materially different certainty.
- Scores are ordinal within a dimension. Do not sum or average the eight dimensions into a total danger score.
- The interaction layer is a reviewed evidence registry, not a mixing checker. It must never generate unreviewed pair conclusions algorithmically.
- The interactive comparison explorer may compare published axis values for any two substances, but it must never auto-generate an editorial conclusion for a pair that lacks a reviewed comparison page.
